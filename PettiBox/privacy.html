Privacy Policy for PettiBox

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

PettiBox is made by an independent developer who publishes apps under the name GhostGram Labs. GhostGram Labs is not a company. This Privacy Policy explains how PettiBox handles information when you use the app.

1. Overview

PettiBox is an offline-first Android app that helps you save links, notes, images, screenshots, PDFs, text, and files so you can organize, read, search, and find them later.

PettiBox does not require an account for normal use. Signing in with Google is optional and is used only for Google Drive backup, restore, and sync.

PettiBox does not run any server that stores your saved content. There are no PettiBox servers, no ads, and no analytics or tracking by the developer (Google ML Kit's own technical diagnostics are described in section 3).

2. Information You Save in the App

PettiBox stores the content you choose to save, which may include:

  • Links and URLs
  • Text and notes
  • Images and screenshots
  • PDFs and files
  • Collections, tags, pins, favorites, archive status, and reminders
  • Readable offline copies of saved articles
  • Text extracted from images or PDFs for search
  • Link metadata such as titles, descriptions, and preview images

This information is stored on your device unless you choose to export, share, back up, or sync it.

3. Text Recognition (OCR)

PettiBox reads text in your saved images, screenshots, and PDFs on your device, using Google ML Kit and Tesseract, so you can search for it. Your images, PDFs and the text in them are not sent anywhere for this.

Google ML Kit, which is part of Google Play services, sends Google limited technical information so it can work and be improved: your device model, Android version, the app's name and version, how fast text recognition ran, error codes, and (for some languages) a random per-install identifier that is not meant to identify you. Google encrypts it in transit and does not share it with third parties. It never includes your images, PDFs or text. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

For some languages, PettiBox downloads a language model the first time you turn that language on. These models are downloaded from the public Tesseract project on GitHub. Only the model file is downloaded. None of your content is uploaded.

4. Link Previews and Offline Article Copies

When you save a link, PettiBox may connect to that webpage to fetch its title, description, and preview image, and to keep a readable offline copy of the article on your device.

The website receives normal connection information, such as your IP address and user agent, as it would if you opened the page in a browser. PettiBox does not use this information to track you.

5. Reminders and Notifications

PettiBox stores reminders on your device and uses Android notifications and alarms to remind you at the time you choose. An optional weekly nudge is also created on your device. Reminder data is not sent to any server.

6. App Lock and Locked Collections

You can lock PettiBox, or individual collections, with your fingerprint, face, or phone PIN. This uses Android's own screen-lock and biometric system. PettiBox never receives or stores your fingerprint, face data, or PIN.

7. Permissions

PettiBox uses the following permissions:

  • Internet: used for link previews, offline article copies, OCR language downloads, and Google Drive backup and sync when you turn them on.
  • Notifications: used only for reminders and the optional weekly nudge.
  • Exact alarms and run at startup: used to deliver reminders at the time you chose, including after the phone restarts.

PettiBox does not ask for access to all your photos or files. When you save a file, you pick it with Android's file picker or share it from another app, and PettiBox receives only that item.

8. Backup and Restore

PettiBox can create backup files containing your saved items, collections, tags, notes, attachments, reminders, offline articles, extracted text, and related metadata. You can also export your links as a CSV file.

Backups are created only by PettiBox's backup features, on your device and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive.

9. Google Drive Backup, Restore, and Sync

Google Drive features are optional. If you turn them on, PettiBox asks for access to your Google Drive using the limited drive.file permission, which only lets PettiBox see files and folders that PettiBox itself created.

PettiBox uses this access only to:

  • Back up your PettiBox library, nightly or when you ask, to My Drive / PettiBox / Backups
  • Restore your library from those backups
  • Sync your library between your own Android phones and tablets through My Drive / PettiBox / Sync, if you turn on sync

Sync files contain your saved PettiBox content, including links, notes, collections, tags, reminders, photos, files, and related metadata, so that your other devices can download them. Sync happens directly between your devices and your own Google Drive. It does not pass through any PettiBox or GhostGram Labs server, and the developer cannot see your saves.

PettiBox does not access, read, modify, delete, or collect any other files in your Google Drive.

Your Google Drive data is not sent to the developer. PettiBox does not sell, share, or use your Google Drive data for advertising, tracking, or training AI models.

You can turn off backup or sync in PettiBox at any time, delete the PettiBox folder from your Google Drive, and remove PettiBox's access from your Google Account permissions page.

PettiBox's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

10. PettiBox for Chrome

The optional PettiBox extension for Google Chrome saves the page you are on when you click it, and lets you search and edit your saves. It reads only the tab you choose to save (its address, title and icon). It signs in with your Google account through Chrome and syncs with your PettiBox apps through the same My Drive / PettiBox / Sync folder, using the limited drive.file permission. Your library is kept in Chrome's storage on your computer and in your own Google Drive. Nothing is sent to the developer. Removing the extension deletes its copy from Chrome.

11. Importing Bookmarks

When you import a bookmark file (for example from Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari, Pocket, or Raindrop.io), PettiBox reads the file you pick on your device. The file is not uploaded anywhere.

12. Sharing and Exporting

When you choose to share or export content, it is sent only to the app or service you select.

13. Archive and Deletion

Archived items stay on your device until you restore or delete them. Deleted items are kept in Recently deleted for 30 days and then removed. When items are permanently deleted, PettiBox removes the related records and files from your device. If sync is on, the deletion is passed on to your other devices, and files no longer used by any save are cleaned out of the PettiBox sync folder.

14. Data Collection

PettiBox does not collect your saved content, usage data, or personal information on any server, and does not sell personal information. The developer never receives your saves, your Google account details or your Drive files. The only data that leaves your device without you asking is the technical ML Kit information described in section 3, which goes to Google.

15. Third-Party Services

PettiBox uses third-party libraries for app features, including file handling, background work, image loading, link previews, text recognition (Google ML Kit and Tesseract), and Google Sign-In and Google Drive for the optional backup and sync. These are used only to provide those features.

Installs, updates and the optional in-app rating prompt are handled by Google Play. Google may give the developer anonymous, aggregated statistics and crash reports under the Google Privacy Policy; these do not contain your saves.

16. Data Retention

Your saved content stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall PettiBox. Backup and sync files stay in your Google Drive until you delete them.

17. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), Brazil's LGPD and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act give you rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal data and to object to its use. Your PettiBox library is on your own devices and in your own Google Drive, so you can see, export (Backup or CSV export), change or delete all of it yourself at any time. The developer holds no copy, does not sell or share personal information, and makes no automated decisions about you. For questions or complaints, email the address below; you can also contact your local data protection authority.

18. Children's Privacy

PettiBox is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

19. Security

Your saved content stays on your device unless you choose backup, sync, export, or sharing. Backup and sync files in Google Drive are protected by your Google account. Keep backups only in places you trust.
