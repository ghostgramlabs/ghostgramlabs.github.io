Why PettiBox?

Every day you come across things worth keeping: an article for later, a recipe from Instagram, a screenshot of a receipt, a PDF from work, a product you might buy. They end up scattered across browser bookmarks, chat apps, your gallery and your downloads folder — and when you need them, they're gone.

PettiBox is a bookmark manager and read-it-later app that gives them one home. Collections and tags keep it tidy, offline search with OCR finds anything in seconds, and reminders make sure "later" actually comes.

When Pocket shut down, a lot of people lost the habit along with the app. PettiBox keeps your library on your own device — and in your own Google Drive if you choose — so no service shutdown can take it away. Import your old Pocket export and pick up where you left off.

Google Drive usage

Google Drive access is optional and used only for PettiBox backup, restore and sync. PettiBox can only see the files it created, never the rest of your Drive. See the Privacy Policy for details.

FAQ

Is PettiBox a good Pocket alternative?
Yes. It imports your old Pocket export file, keeps readable offline copies of saved articles, and stores the library on your own device (and optionally your own Google Drive), so no service shutdown can take it away.
Can I import my Chrome or browser bookmarks?
Yes. Import bookmark files exported from Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari, plus HTML and CSV exports from apps like Pocket and Raindrop.io. Folders become collections, tags carry over, duplicates are skipped. You can export your links as CSV any time.
Can PettiBox search inside screenshots and PDFs?
Yes. On-device OCR makes text inside screenshots, images and PDFs searchable, in English and other Latin-alphabet languages plus 24 more, including Hindi, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Bengali and Tamil.
Can I read saved articles offline?
Yes. PettiBox keeps a clean, readable copy of each saved article on your phone, so it's there without internet even if the page later changes or disappears.
Does it sync between my phone and tablet?
Yes, if you turn it on. Your library stays in step through a PettiBox folder in your own Google Drive. There are no PettiBox servers, and PettiBox only sees the files it created.
Is PettiBox free?
Yes — free on Google Play, with no ads, no subscription and no account. It's a hobby project by an independent developer, and the code is open source under Apache 2.0. GhostGram Labs is not a company, and the app earns nothing.
