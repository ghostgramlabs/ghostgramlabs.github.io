DirectServe: Cast & File Share

Watch your phone's videos, photos and music on a TV, laptop or tablet, mirror your screen, and move files between your phone and an iPhone, PC or Mac — over your own WiFi or hotspot. The other screen only needs a web browser: no app to install, no cable, no account, no cloud.

Download Free on Google Play

Your phone, on any screen

Pick what to share, tap Start sharing, scan the QR code on the other device. That's the whole thing.

Cast Videos to a TV or Laptop

MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM, AVI and more play straight from your phone — no copying first. 4K, HDR and HEVC play directly where the browser supports them. Resume, Previous / Next, and double-tap to skip.

Mirror Your Screen

Show your phone screen live in any browser, with sound from apps that allow it. Handy for classes, meetings, app demos and showing photos to a room.

Files Both Ways

Send to an iPhone, PC, Mac or another phone — one file, a selection, or everything as a ZIP. Upload from a computer back to your phone, approving each transfer. PDFs, Office files, ZIPs and APKs too.

One QR Code for Everyone

Everyone on the same WiFi or hotspot scans the same code and gets their own page — to watch, browse or download what they want, all at once. No app for them, no sign-up.

Smart TVs, VLC & Kodi

Turn on DLNA and smart TVs, TV boxes, VLC and Kodi on the same network find your phone as a media server. Photos get a full-screen slideshow; music plays as a playlist.

Private & Offline

Works on your local WiFi or hotspot with no internet. No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking. Add a PIN, see who's connected, block a device, stop with one tap.

Why I made DirectServe

Getting one video from an Android phone onto a TV, a laptop or a friend's iPhone is still oddly hard. AirDrop doesn't talk to Android. Casting needs the right stick and the right app. Cables need the right port. Cloud drives want an account, an upload and good internet. Most sharing apps need to be installed on both devices.

DirectServe takes a simpler route: your phone becomes a small streaming and file server on your own network. Anything with a browser — a smart TV, a Windows PC, a Mac, a Chromebook, an iPad or an iPhone — opens a link and gets its own page to watch, browse, download or upload.

Because it all stays on your WiFi or hotspot, it works on a train, in a hotel room or at a campsite with no internet, and your files never go through a cloud server. DirectServe is a personal side project, not a company product: free, no ads, no in-app purchases, open source, and available in 23 languages. It earns nothing.

FAQ

How do I play videos from my Android phone on a TV without a cable?
Start sharing in DirectServe and open the address it shows in the TV's web browser, or turn on DLNA so the TV finds your phone as a media server. Videos play straight from the phone over your WiFi. Nothing is installed on the TV.
How do I send files from Android to iPhone without AirDrop?
Start sharing, then scan the QR code with the iPhone camera or open the link in Safari. Photos, videos and files download directly over WiFi or hotspot — no iOS app, no cloud, no cable.
Can I mirror my Android screen to a laptop?
Yes. DirectServe mirrors your screen live to any browser on the same network, with sound from apps that allow it. Some apps block screen and sound capture.
Does it work without internet?
Yes. It works on your local WiFi or your phone's hotspot and needs no internet connection. Files stay on your own network, with no account and no cloud upload.
Will MKV, 4K or HEVC videos play?
MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM, AVI, TS and more play straight from the phone, and 4K, HDR and HEVC play directly in browsers that support them. If a browser can't play a file as it is, DirectServe converts it on the phone when you press Play and starts the video while the rest is still being prepared. Very large or unusual videos take longer, depending on your phone.
Can several people download at the same time?
Yes. Everyone scans the same QR code and gets their own page on their own device, so a family, class or group can each watch or download what they want at once.
Can other devices send files to my phone?
Yes. A laptop or another phone can upload photos, videos and documents from its browser. You approve each transfer, and you can protect the share with a PIN.
Is it free?
Yes — free on Google Play, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no account. It runs on Android 8.0 and later, and the code is open source under Apache 2.0.
